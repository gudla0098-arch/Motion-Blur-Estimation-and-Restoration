THE THEORY

From blur to clarity.

Motion blur occurs when a camera or subject moves while the shutter is open. Restoration estimates the blur process and attempts to reverse its effect. Results depend on the accuracy of the blur model and the information retained in the input image.

01 / Estimate

Define motion direction, length and point-spread function.

02 / Restore

Apply Wiener deconvolution to reduce modeled blur.

03 / Evaluate

Inspect the output. Severe blur and noise can limit recovery.
